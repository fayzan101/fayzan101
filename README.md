I lead a team of 10 across full stack, frontend, backend, and AI, and spend most of my time shipping production systems — from Play Store apps to live e-commerce stores to backend infrastructure running in the wild.

- 🔭 Currently deep in **DevOps** — CI/CD pipelines, Docker, Kubernetes, and observability with Prometheus/Grafana
